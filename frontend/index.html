About Our Platform

Electronic waste (e-waste) is one of the fastest-growing waste problems in the world. Old phones, laptops, batteries and appliances contain harmful materials such as lead and mercury, but also valuable metals that can be reused.

The E-Waste Management System connects people with recycling services. Users can submit their e-waste details and request pickups, while administrators review, approve, collect and recycle items safely.

  • ✔ Easy online registration and request tracking
  • ✔ Safe and responsible recycling
  • ✔ Transparent pickup status updates
